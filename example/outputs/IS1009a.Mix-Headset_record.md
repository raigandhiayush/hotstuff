# Meeting Record

## Summary

The project team met to introduce themselves, review the project plan and design goals for a new television remote control, and discuss initial ideas such as multifunctionality and findability features. The team also reviewed financial targets including selling price, production cost, and profit aim, and scheduled the next meeting for 30 minutes later.

## Minutes

### 1. Introductions

Team members introduced themselves and their roles: Francenna as User Interface designer (television remote control), Eileen as Marketing, John V. as Industrial Designer (technical and functional design of user interface), and Betsy as Project Manager.

### 2. Project Goals and Design Parameters

The team discussed the requirements for the new remote control: it must be original, trendy, and user-friendly. Financial targets were established: selling price of 25 Euro, production cost under 12.50 Euro, profit aim of 50 million Euro, and an unlimited international market range.

### 3. Initial Design Ideas and Features

Participants discussed ideas for the remote control, including making it compact, trendy in shape/materials/colors, and multifunctional to control other household machines like temperature, air conditioners, and heating systems. They also discussed ideas for locating a lost remote, such as a light sensor that triggers a signal or light when in a dark spot (like behind a plant or under a newspaper).

### 4. Next Steps and Scheduling

The team reviewed that individual work will be done for functional, conceptual, and detailed designs, followed by group discussions. The next meeting was scheduled for in 30 minutes.
