# Meeting Record

## Summary

Sara, the project manager, led the first team meeting to introduce team members, discuss project plans for developing a new remote control, and review financial targets and design ideas. The team practiced sketching favorite animals on the whiteboard, discussed product pricing and production costs, shared personal experiences with remote controls, and explored design ideas such as incorporating an LCD display, a dual-function button layout, and keeping the product lightweight yet fashionable.

## Minutes

### 1. Team Introductions and Icebreaker

The project manager Sara outlined the agenda. Team members participated in an icebreaker exercise by drawing their favorite animals on the whiteboard and summarizing their characteristics.

### 2. Financial Overview

The team reviewed financial details including a selling price of 25 euros (discussed as approximately 17 pounds), production costs of 12.50 euros, and a profit aim of 50 million euros in the first year.

### 3. Target Market

The product is aimed at an international market (such as Australia and America) and must be user-friendly and accessible to all age groups, including older generations, rather than focusing on a business market.

### 4. Remote Control Experiences and Design Ideas

Members shared experiences with universal remote controls and complex buttons. They discussed design concepts to make the remote user-friendly, including an LCD display with menus, a dual-function layout with basic buttons on the front and advanced options under a cover, large-print options for older users, and using lightweight molded plastic while keeping the company's focus on putting fashion into electronics.
